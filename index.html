What MapYou does

GPS workout tracking

Records running, walking and cycling. Distance, pace, time and elevation, with automatic pause when you stop.

Your route on a map

Every workout is drawn on a map you can revisit, share, or compare with earlier sessions.

Live sharing with friends

Send a link and friends can follow your position in real time while you are out — useful for safety on longer routes.

Clubs, events and challenges

Join a club, take part in distance challenges, and see how you compare with other members.

Works offline

Lose signal mid-run and nothing is lost. Your workout keeps recording and syncs automatically once you are back online.

Health app import

Optionally brings in workouts recorded by Apple Health or Health Connect, so your history stays in one place.

Availability

MapYou is in final testing on iOS and Android. Store links will appear here as soon as the apps are published.

Privacy

  • MapYou does not sell your data and shows no advertising.
  • Location is recorded only while you are actively tracking a workout.
  • Your routes are private by default — you decide what to share and with whom.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, from inside the app.
